# Hidden ingredients and cross-contamination

Reached from step 3 when a food is processed, packaged, or restaurant-style.

## Hidden ingredients to add to the flat list

- **Sauces and condiments**: soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce, Worcestershire,
  hoisin, pesto, mayonnaise, aioli, mustards, dressings.
- **Stocks and bases**: bouillon cubes, stock powder, gravy granules, curry pastes.
- **Spice blends**: za'atar (sesame), garam masala (may contain nuts), curry powder
  (may contain mustard or celery), taco seasoning.
- **Baked and coated**: breadcrumbs, panko, batter, pastry, marzipan, praline, nougat.
- **Desserts**: ice cream, chocolate (may contain milk, nuts, soy), custard, meringue.
- **Drinks**: beer and wine (sulphites), some spirits, flavoured syrups.

## Label language

- "May contain traces of X" — treat as containing X.
- "Produced in a facility that also handles X" — treat as containing X unless the
  person's clinician says otherwise.
- "Natural flavouring", "spices", "modified starch" — unknown; when it could hide an
  allergen, prefer a version with an explicit ingredient list.

## Cross-contamination

- Shared fryers: chips cooked with battered fish or shellfish.
- Shared grills and utensils: the allergen remains after a wipe.
- Buffets and shared serving spoons.
- Nut butters and spreads: a knife used in one jar contaminates the next.

## Substitution pattern

For every flagged ingredient, pick a swap that keeps the dish's role intact:

- soy sauce -> coconut aminos (if soy only) with an allergen check
- butter -> olive oil or a verified dairy-free spread
- breadcrumbs -> crushed rice crackers verified against the profile
- peanut garnish -> toasted pumpkin seeds (only if no seed allergy in the profile)
